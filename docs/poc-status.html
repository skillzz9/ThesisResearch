3What's wrong — per axis

The one diagnostic that matters: every axis fits seen data (0.90–0.97). Nothing left is an architecture problem — each shortfall below is a flavour of "not enough distinct songs."

key — solved for near transpositions, data-thin for wide ones

Passes the criteria outright at ±1 semitone (0.77 acc / 0.85 AUC) thanks to the pitch-class branch. Drops to 0.59 when clashes can be any of ±1–6 semitones: the general rule spans 11 rotation offsets, but babyslakh provides only ~10 clash examples per offset — too thin to learn each case.

Fix: more songs (fills every offset with examples). Not architecture — the branch already computes the right representation.

consonance — at the bar, needs a nudge

0.60–0.61 accuracy, AUC straddling 0.62–0.71. Fits seen at 0.97, so the concept is learned; transfer is limited by hearing dissonance on only ~14 songs' worth of instrument timbres.

Fix: more songs + more timbres. Closest axis to passing; likely the first to clear at scale.

tempo — weakest generalizer

0.55–0.58 unseen vs 0.90 seen. The drift signal is the subtlest of the four (a gradually growing misalignment), and with few songs the model memorises song-specific onset textures instead of the general drift pattern.

Fix: more songs (the GRU already gives it the right representation — it fits seen at 0.90+).

timing — same story as tempo

0.55 unseen vs 0.94 seen. Phase misalignment is learnable (near-perfect on seen) but the decision boundary doesn't transfer across unseen timbres at this song count.

Fix: more songs.

Why we're confident it's data, not the model — the two decisive experiments

1 · The pairs-null: quadrupling pairs (156→626) from the same songs moved pooled unseen accuracy by ~0. If the model were the problem, better features + more examples would have helped. They didn't.

2 · The song-scaling curve: adding songs lifts unseen performance measurably (re-running now with the final architecture). Songs are the lever; babyslakh simply runs out of them at 14.

4The plan that was executed (items 2–5 done; slice no longer required)

One primary move plus three cheap multipliers — then a pre-registered decision gate so the full run is a confirmation, not a gamble.

  1. Slakh2100 slice: 100–200 songs (the primary fix). ~10× the song/timbre diversity, directly attacking every diagnosis above. Only the MIDI/ folders + metadata.yaml are needed (audio is rendered by us) — a small fraction of the full download. Build ≈ 20–40 min on a many-core pod with the existing pipeline.
  2. Multi-soundfont rendering at the slice build. Render different songs through different soundfonts. Timbre diversity is exactly what the overfitting feeds on — this multiplies it per song, and simultaneously narrows the synth→real-audio gap for deployment. One config change.
  3. Keep the widened (±1–6) key negatives. The honest task. The slice supplies the ~15× more examples per rotation offset that key's general rule needs.
  4. Train longer at slice scale. 15 epochs was time-boxed for laptop iteration; at ~5,000+ pairs use 25–30 epochs (compute is cheap on the pod).
  5. Do NOT touch the architecture. All four axes fit seen at 0.90+. Of five architecture interventions tried, the two that worked (tempo GRU, pitch-class branch) gave an axis a representation matched to its signal; the three generic tweaks all backfired. There is no representation problem left to fix.
